# UX/UI review — 2026-10-10

Done by reading the code (full inventory of the tab bar, the status bars and
all the overlays) and on a screenshot of the Kova window in real use (6 tabs, 2 panes
visible). The screenshot was deleted after reading. Nothing is implemented: these are
proposals, sorted by gain.

Note: the running Kova shows `v1.13.0` in the tab bar while the installed bundle
is 1.14.0 (`/Applications/Kova.app/Contents/Info.plist`). What follows describes the
code of `main`, which may differ slightly from what is on screen.

## 1. Cmd+P: typing should filter

**What it is**: the switcher lists all tabs and their panes over three columns. You
navigate it with the arrows. Any key other than the expected ones is ignored
(`src/window/switcher.rs:927`, `_ => return`).

**Why it matters**: with twenty or so panes, finding a pane named "api" means reading three
columns and moving down with the arrows, when typing `ap` would be enough. It is the most
frequent gesture of the day (Cmd+P is the main navigation).

**Proposal**: each typed letter is added to a filter (pane title, tab name, cwd,
Claude session name), the list shrinks live, Enter opens the first row.
Conflict to resolve: `u` and `b` are toggles today ("unread" list, bookmarks,
`switcher.rs:815, 827`). Move them to `Tab`/`Cmd+U` and `Cmd+B` (the latter already exists elsewhere).

## 2. The status bars say too many non-actionable things

**What it is**: each pane has its bar (`#id`, cwd, branch, process, title, I/O `↑↓`,
date+time of last activity), and the window has a global bar (help, tab name,
columns, `⊟5/5`, `●6`, `✳1`, `▶33`, total I/O, RSS, time). On the screenshot, the time appears
three times (two panes + global bar), and the Claude Code bar just above also shows
model, cost and context.

**Why it matters**: the useful counters (`●` unread, `✳` Claude working, `⊟`
minimized) drown in figures nobody ever reads to act on: `▶33` (child processes),
`↑4.3M ↓189.2M` (bytes exchanged), `63.0M` (Kova's RAM). The meaning of `▶`, `⊟`, `●` can only
be discovered on hover (tooltips, `src/renderer/mod.rs:2078-2136`).

**Proposal**:
- Global bar: keep `●N ✳N ⊟N` and the time; move I/O, RSS and `▶N` to the
  Cmd+Shift+I report, which already exists for that.
- Make `●N` clickable (opens Cmd+Shift+J) and `✳N` clickable (Cmd+P on the sessions that
  are working), as `⊟` already is (`mod.rs:2137`).
- Pane bar: remove date and I/O by default, keep `#id`, cwd, branch, process.

## 3. Tab bar

**What can be seen and read in the code**:
- **No overflow handling**: past the minimum width (4 cells), tabs
  keep going to the right outside the bar (`mod.rs:2163-2169`). No scroll, no chevron.
- **Title cut at 25 characters without `…`** (`mod.rs:2212-2225`): you cannot tell the end
  is missing.
- **Indicators are distinguished only by color**: orange `●` (bell), green `●`
  (command finished), yellow `▶` (process running) (`mod.rs:2199-2207`). On a green tab,
  the green `●` blends in; the code switches the indicator to white when the contrast is too
  low (`mod.rs:2253-2259`), which erases precisely the color information.
- **The active tab** is marked by a white bar of 4 physical px, so 2 pt on Retina
  (`mod.rs:2190`), on backgrounds that are already saturated.
- The tab color menu says "Aucune" in French, everything else is in English
  (`src/window/tabs.rs:72-120`).

**Proposal**: `…` ellipsis on cut titles; distinct shapes rather than colors
alone (`●` bell, `✓` finished, `▶` running); narrower tabs then horizontal scrolling
with arrows when they no longer fit; active bar in logical points (×scale);
"None" in the menu.

## 4. Overlays do not behave the same

Five keyboard lists (Cmd+P, Cmd+Shift+F, Cmd+O, the "send/merge" pickers, Cmd+F), five
behaviors:
- **Mouse**: click works in Cmd+P and Cmd+F only; nothing in the global search,
  Cmd+O and the pickers (`src/window/mod.rs:864-874`). Scroll only works in Cmd+P.
- **The shortcut that opens does not close**: Cmd+P and Cmd+Shift+F do not close their own
  overlay, while Cmd+F and Cmd+Shift+/ do.
- **Deletion**: Cmd+⌫ asks for confirmation in Cmd+P (bookmark), not in Cmd+O
  (`src/window/recent_projects_overlay.rs`).
- **Style**: blue headers in Cmd+P, yellow in the search; veil at 0.9 here, 0.85 there;
  three ways of drawing text (overlay atlas, base ×1.3, base).
- **↑↓** selects a row everywhere except in Cmd+F, where it goes up the history.

**Proposal**: a single "overlay list" component (selection, scroll, click, hover,
closing with Esc or with its own shortcut, headers, help line with colored keys
as in Cmd+P), onto which the five are rewired.

## 5. Readability of the default colors

**What it is**: the ANSI palette is hard-coded (`src/terminal/parser.rs:908-927`). WCAG contrast
computed on the default background `[0.1, 0.1, 0.12]`: blue 2.9:1, "bright
black" grey 3.0:1, red 3.4:1. The recommended minimum for readable text is 4.5:1.

**Why it matters**: blue and dark grey are the colors of paths, links,
comments and the "dim" text of Claude Code and TUIs (lazygit, btop): these are
texts people actually read.

**Proposal**: raise the blue (towards `100,140,255`) and the grey (towards `128,128,128`),
make the 16-color palette and the selection color (`mod.rs:743`, not configurable)
adjustable in `[colors]`.

## 6. Sizes in physical pixels

Several sizes do not follow the screen scale and therefore change appearance between the Retina
screen and an external screen: focused pane border (2.0, documented "in points" in
`src/config.rs:136-137` but drawn in pixels, `mod.rs:1770`), active tab bar,
separators, edge flash, golden jump outline (`mod.rs:1133, 1164, 1247`). Same family as
the bug already diagnosed in `notes/screen-switch-resize.md` ("the number of columns changes on screen switch").

**Proposal**: multiply everything by the scale in one place, together with that fix.

## 7. Discoverability

- **macOS menu bar almost empty**: only "Check for Updates…" and a "Close
  Window" with no action (`src/app.rs:1533-1574`). Putting the commands into menus (Shell,
  View, Window) with their shortcuts gives for free the command search of
  macOS Help > Search, and a place to find a forgotten shortcut.
- **The Cmd+Shift+/ help** hard-codes `cmd+1-9`, `cmd+shift+i`, `ctrl+l`, `cmd+click`
  (`mod.rs:2643-2701`) and is not rebuilt on config reload (cache never cleared
  in `apply_config`, `mod.rs:775-810`). It does not list the keys internal to the overlays
  (`u`, `b`, `Tab`, Cmd+↑↓).

## 8. Small defects noticed along the way

- The "merge window" picker is titled "Send Tab to Window" (`src/window/tick.rs:355-361`).
- The Cmd+J banner shows "Tier 1", "Tier 2": internal vocabulary. "Unread output",
  "Idle Claude session" are enough.
- The comment `src/pane.rs:1672` still mentions a 24 px bar for minimized
  panes, which no longer exists; `roadmap.md` describes it too.
